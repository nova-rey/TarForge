# TarForge — Intended Use & Legal Boundary

TarForge is an open-source packaging utility designed exclusively for working with **open-source operating systems** or other content that the user is legally permitted to repackage.

TarForge does **not**:
- fetch, mirror, or distribute operating system images  
- validate OS licensing  
- assist in cloning proprietary systems  
- analyze or classify input filesystem data  
- enforce redistributability of user-provided content  

Users are solely responsible for ensuring that any OS images or filesystems they provide to TarForge are used in accordance with their licenses.

TarForge is **not intended** for:
- cloning proprietary operating systems  
- redistributing licensed or paid content  
- circumventing installation mechanisms  
- replicating systems tied to hardware activation or subscription services  

If users misuse the tool for such purposes, they do so at their own risk and against project intent.  
The maintainers provide no support for such use cases.
